# Research & White Papers

This index collects the current public research and architecture papers associated with Randy Webb, Lucy AIOS, and the broader P.E.L.A.P.H. research direction.

These documents describe an evolving research and engineering program. Architecture proposals, internal validation results, simulations, and research hypotheses should be read according to the status stated inside each paper.

## Publication note

The GitHub editions are readable text transcriptions of the authored papers. Formatted PDF editions remain the preferred publication format for layout, figures, tables, and pagination.

## Research direction

Together, these papers cover several connected areas:

- governed agentic AI and human authority
- evidence, provenance, and verification
- security observation and bounded execution
- planetary-system analysis and hazard intelligence
- AI infrastructure and planetary pressure
- institutional accountability and consequence

The work is active and subject to revision as implementation evidence, datasets, architecture, and terminology mature.
